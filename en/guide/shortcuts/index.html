Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcuts that differ by platform are given both ways: ⌘ and ⌥ on macOS, Ctrl and Alt on Windows and Linux.

Anywhere in the window

Link to the “Anywhere in the window” section
Keys What they do
Space Play or pause, like the play key on the play bar, wherever the focus is. Except in a text field, while a context menu is open, or with a modifier held
⌘ K (Ctrl K on Windows and Linux) Focus the search field
⌘ , (Ctrl , on Windows and Linux) Open the settings
Esc In the search field: clear the text, or leave the field when it is empty

Track list

Link to the “Track list” section
Keys What they do
↑ ↓ Move to the previous or next row and select only that row
Home End Move to the first or last row
Shift with an arrow key Select every row from the anchor to the focused row
Enter Play the focused row
Shift-click Select a range
⌘-click (Ctrl-click on Windows and Linux) Add a row to the selection or take it out

Context menu

Link to the “Context menu” section
Keys What they do
Shift F10, or the menu key Open the context menu
↑ ↓ Move between the items you can use
Enter Run the item
Esc Close the menu and return the focus

Controls

Link to the “Controls” section
Keys What they do
← → Change a segmented control’s position; move a fader
Enter Toggle the focused switch, or activate the focused key or sidebar entry
⌥ ↑ ⌥ ↓ (Alt ↑ Alt ↓ on Windows and Linux) In the lyric sources list, move the focused source up or down one place
