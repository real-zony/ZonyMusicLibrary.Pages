About

ZonyMusicLibrary is a local music library for Windows, macOS and Linux. It organizes music files, matches covers and lyrics, and keeps playlists as the one source.

Design

Link to the “Design” section

The interface is designed the way Braun and Dieter Rams designed: it should be like a Braun hi-fi on a bookshelf. A light body, physical keys, one orange key, a few lamps and a speaker grille. Less, but better.

Dieter Rams’s principles of good design become these concrete rules in the app:

Good design is… In this app
Useful Every element has a job. No decorative color areas, illustrations, gradients or textures; the only “decoration” is the grille, and it marks where the sound comes from.
Understandable Controls look like what they do: what you press is a raised key, what slides is a knob in a groove, what flips is a switch. Lamps and words state what is going on.
Unobtrusive The body is a neutral off-white; the interface steps back so covers and music lead.
Honest No imitation of materials that are not there: no wood grain, brushed metal or leather. Shadows only show what is truly raised or recessed, and the light always comes from straight above.
Aesthetic Beauty comes from proportion, alignment and space, not from adding things.
Long-lasting No trends: no colored glass blobs, no neon, no large gradients.
Thorough Corners, spacing and type sizes all come from the tokens; the same kind of thing looks the same on every page.
As little design as possible The interface has one color, and it means sound. Whatever can go without hurting understanding, goes.

This site uses the same design system: the orange key of the player on the home page really makes sound, and its level lamps show the sound as measured.
